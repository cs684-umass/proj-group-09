Here is the SQL query to answer this question:
```sql
SELECT MAX(T2.COST) 
FROM d_icd_diagnoses T1 
JOIN diagnoses_icd T2 ON T1.ICD9_CODE = T2.ICD9_CODE 
JOIN cost T3 ON T2.HADM_ID = T3.HADM_ID 
WHERE T1.SHORT_TITLE = 'comp-oth vasc dev/graft' 
  AND T3.CHARGETIME >= (CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL 1 YEAR);
